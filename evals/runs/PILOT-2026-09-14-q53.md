# Retrieval depth changes a verdict — Q53's ten-pair pilot, 2026-09-14

**What this is.** [`PREREGISTRATION-Q53.md`](PREREGISTRATION-Q53.md) and its three amendments, run.
Four retrieval write-ups measured whether a relevant document is *reachable*. **This is the first
measurement in this repository of whether the model reads it and answers differently**, and the
limitation T6.4 §5 named and three write-ups inherited is now answered rather than restated.

**The result.** **2 of 10 pairs produced a different `fault_class`. In both, `k5-flag2` is correct
and `k3-flag0` is not.**

**What it cost.** **$14.43** of a hard $25 ceiling, against a registered prediction of under $15.
Six dry runs before it spent **$2.95** and bought six defects, four of which would have spoiled this
run and one of which would have mis-stated its conclusion. §6 is that list and is the part of this
document worth reading twice.

**What it decides.** Per §3.2: **nothing is adopted, and Q53 does not close.** `TEXT_NORMALISATION`
stays 0 and `ContextSettings.retrieval_k` stays 3. One or more differing was registered in advance
as the outcome that funds a larger measurement, and that measurement — **30–40 pairs, $43–$58 at
this run's measured $1.44 per pair** — is now warranted and is **Q58**.

Evidence: `docs/evidence/q53-depth-pilot/q53-pilot.json`, `q53-pilot.log`.

**7 of 10 against 9 of 10 is not a second finding.** The eight agreeing pairs contribute nothing to

## 2. The predictions

**Six held. One was retired before the run by Amendment 1, and would have failed.**

| # | registered | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | at least one pair produces a different `fault_class` | **held** — 2 of 10 |
| 2 | no more than three do | **held** — 2 |
| 3 | where a verdict changes, `k = 5` is right at least as often | **held** — 2–0 |
| 4 | no pair differs only in `remediation_class` | **retired** by Amendment 1; observationally it **fails** — pair 8, `restart` → `config_revert` with the class unmoved |
| 5 | total spend under $15 | **held** — $14.43, by $0.57 |
| 6 | at least 8 of 10 pairs complete | **held** — 10 of 10, **for a reason unrelated to its own arithmetic**; see §4 |
| 7 | `k = 5`'s documents are a superset of `k = 3`'s in fewer than half the pairs | **held** — **0 of 10** |

**Prediction 3 is the one that mattered and it is the one this pilot was most at risk from.** It was
registered because the opposite result — more context making the answer worse — is the distraction
risk, and is the single finding that would have settled Q53 against the change outright. It did not
happen. That is not evidence the change is good; 2 of 2 in one direction has a one-in-four chance
under a coin flip, and this pilot cannot and does not claim significance.

**Prediction 7 held at 0 of 10, and the margin matters more than the verdict.** Q48 established the
arms are not nested — each applies its own `LIMIT k` and `fuse` takes `limit=k`, so a deeper cut
reorders rather than extends. Ten pairs, zero nested:

|---|---:|---:|---|
| `ad-memory-squeeze` | 3 | **3** | no |
| `cart-bad-image-tag` | 3 | 5 | no |
| `cart-dependency-latency` | 2 | 4 | no |
| `cart-redis-misconfig` | 3 | 5 | no |
| `frauddetection-memory-squeeze` | 2 | 4 | no |

**Look at pair 1**, one of the two that changed the verdict: `k3 = 3`, `k5 = 3`. The deeper arm
returned **the same number of documents, and different ones**. So whatever moved that verdict, it
was not more context. It was **different** context — which means the registration's own framing of
the risk, *"a larger `k` puts more retrieved text in front of the model"*, describes the wrong
mechanism. The joint change is a different retrieval, not a deeper one, and Q58 should be designed
against that rather than against a depth intuition.

## 3. What a difference here is allowed to mean

**Three things can move a verdict between the arms of this design, and only one of them is
retrieval.** All three are recorded in the registration's amendments, before the run:

1. **Retrieval** — the change under test.
2. **The rejection** between the arms (Amendment 1). The second arm is reachable only through
   `REJECTED`, and the rejection enters the proposer's brief.
3. **Time** (Amendment 3). The fault keeps running while the first arm investigates, the incident
   keeps absorbing firings, and `faultline-investigate` re-derives triage every invocation (Q55), so
   the second arm sees a later and usually larger incident.

**So a reported difference means *retrieval, or the rejection, or twenty minutes of extra world*,
and this sentence is the one that reports it.** Alternation — five pairs each way — stops (2) and
(3) being confounded *with the arm*; it does not remove them.

**The asymmetry runs the right way and that is why the result is usable.** Both confounds can only
*add* differences. A run of 0 of 10 would have been strengthened by them; 2 of 10 is weakened by
them. This pilot found differences, so its finding is the weaker branch — which is exactly why
§3.2 spends nothing on it beyond funding a proper measurement.

### 3.1 The rejection contamination is smaller than T6.3 predicted

Amendment 1 narrowed the outcome to `fault_class` because T6.3 measured a rejection changing the
next proposal **2 of 2, both abstentions**. Against that, this run:

**`remediation_class` moved in 3 of 10 pairs, and in 2 of those the fault class moved underneath
it.** Only pair 8 moved remediation alone.

So the contamination is bounded here at roughly **1 in 10, not 2 in 2**. The likely reason is stated
in `depthpilot.REJECTION_REASON`'s own docstring: T6.3's reasons were substantive operator reports
written to move the proposal, and this one is deliberately inert and identical across all ten pairs.

**Amendment 1 is not retracted.** Narrowing to `fault_class` was correct on the evidence available
before the run, and a decision rule chosen after seeing that the contamination was mild would be the
thing this repository refuses everywhere else. What changes is the input to Q58's design: it now has
a measured contamination rate instead of T6.3's worst case, and a cleaner design may be affordable.

## 4. Prediction 6 held by accident, and that is worth more than the prediction

Amendment 2 recorded, before the run, that prediction 6's arithmetic was wrong: it used the
per-**run** discard rate of 16.7% where a pair needs both halves, so the expectation was 6.9
complete pairs and P(≥8) was about 13%. One re-attempt per scenario was registered to restore n = 10.

**Ten of ten completed. Both the prediction and the amendment's reasoning were wrong.**

- **No investigation failed.** Zero. The discard rate the retry was sized for did not appear once in
  twenty investigations.
- **Nine of ten first attempts were refused by the gate**, and the retry was consumed entirely by
  that. Without Amendment 2, **one pair would have completed** and this run would have reported n = 1.

The refusals name two causes and the driver's own settle constant is the reason for both: the gate
refuses while a container has been up under 300s, and while the previous incident sits inside the
orchestrator's 300s settle window. The driver waits 300s from its own revert, but the previous
incident only *resolves* some seconds later and its 300s starts then. Observed remaining waits:
**75s, 125s, 155s, 207s, 51s, 30s, 72s, 141s, 91s.**

`SETTLE_SECONDS = 300` mirrors `sweep.py` and is the wrong constant for a driver that injects
immediately after its own revert. **Queued as Q56.** Until it lands, the re-attempt registered for
investigation failures is not available for investigation failures, and a real one would lose its
pair.

## 5. The world lies to the agent about its own past runs

**Not a Q53 finding, and the most consequential thing this run surfaced.**

Every `faultline-inject start` and `stop` writes a change record, and those records persist across
runs. By pair 1 the change log held four update/revert cycles on cartservice's hotfix tag from
earlier dry runs, and `k3-flag0` built a confident, coherent and **entirely false** root cause from
them:

> *"cartservice was repeatedly redeployed by automation: eight image-reference events form four
> update/revert pairs toggling the same hotfix tag ... after ~22 quiet hours."*

The true fault was a memory cap on adservice. Pair 2 did the same thing (*"nine times, five updates
and four reverts"*), pair 8 reasoned about a shaper the log said was detached 1.7h earlier, and by
pair 9 the agent had noticed the problem itself:

> *"Error onset actually falls between 05:43 and 07:27, overlapping the cartservice fault-injection
> window; whether those injections left residue is untested."*

The records are real events and the agent is reading them correctly. The defect is that **a
sequential benchmark run leaves evidence that later runs read as the world's history**, which makes
every scenario after the first in a session harder in a way nothing measures or reports. **Queued as
Q57.** It touches every sweep this repository has run, not only this pilot.

## 6. What the six dry runs bought, for $2.95

Listed because the pilot's credibility rests on them and because five of the six were found **after**
money had been spent on a run that was already unsalvageable.

| # | found | cost | would have |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | queried a `proposals` table that does not exist | $0.60 | produced no verdicts at all |
| 2 | no settle between pairs | $0.00 | completed pair 1, refused 2–10, reported n = 1 |
| 3 | the second arm is illegal — `INVESTIGABLE` has no door from `proposing` | $0.79 | lost every pair's second arm, ten times |
| 4 | the reject route is unmounted without `FAULTLINE_EXECUTOR_TOKEN_KEY` | $0.00 | 404'd the rejection after each first arm was paid for |
| 5 | a failed arm's spend was invisible to the ceiling | $0.12 | let a $25 budget overrun silently |
| 6 | **the driver printed Q53's conclusion from an unfinished run** | $1.44 | closed Q53 at "87% confidence" on whatever n it reached |

**Defect 6 is the worst of them and it cost the most to find**, because it is the only one that
fails while looking like a success. A run of three pairs with no differences would have printed
*"this closes Q53 ... at 87% confidence"* into the artifact, the report, and anything quoted from
them. `closure_reading()` now computes confidence from the actual `n` and refuses the closure below
the registered ten.

## 8. What this does not settle

- **Whether `k = 5` is better.** Ten pairs cannot, two differences cannot, and §3.2 forbids reading
  the direction as a rate. That is Q58.
- **Whether retrieval caused either difference.** §3 lists three candidate causes and this design
  cannot separate them. It can only say the confounds inflate rather than suppress.
- **Whether depth is even the right frame.** §2's nesting table says the arms retrieve *different*
  documents, not more of the same, and pair 1 changed a verdict with an equal document count. Q58
  should be designed against that finding, not against the depth intuition Q53's row was written
  from.
- **Anything about holdout scenarios.** Dev only, deliberately.
- **Whether the floor is cleared.** ADR-0041 is unchanged and nothing here touches it: a floor met
  because the depth moved to meet it is not a floor that was cleared.
