# Checkout orders hang mid-flow behind slow cart storage operations

## What was visible, in order

The page came from checkoutservice and loadgenerator together, critical, blast radius ten services. The obvious reading — checkoutservice is broken — did not survive first contact. Error ratio for checkoutservice in the window averaged about 1.4% with a brief peak near 27%, against a baseline mean of about 11.6% and peak near 67%, and the comparison flagged no sustained departure. The step change is real but points downward. That killed three hypotheses at once: an error surge at checkoutservice, a persistent downstream failure surfacing as sustained error status, and an upward step at the window boundary.

Logs, pulled next, were all info severity: no errors, no warnings, no restart lines. PlaceOrder intake continued at a steady cadence through 23:42:47, so the process was alive and serving. The signal was structural: early lines show a complete per-order sequence of order start, payment confirmation, confirmation email, queue write, while every late-window line stops at order start with no follow-up. Both USD and CAD orders were affected, so it was not currency-scoped, and the break sits after intake, not at it.

## Where the time actually goes

Traces were the turn. Typical PlaceOrder traces run 22-25ms end to end with every downstream leg at a few milliseconds. One outlier ran 640ms, and the two longest spans in it are cartservice's storage operations: GetCart's HGET at roughly 301ms and EmptyCart's HMSET at roughly 305ms, each carrying essentially its whole duration as self-time. The checkout parent spans inflate to ~303ms and ~307ms purely by waiting; checkoutservice's own self-time is under a millisecond. The tool's own hop attribution independently named the GetCart-to-HGET hop as about 47% of the trace. GetCart sits before the payment step, exactly where the log sequence truncates.

Dead ends worth keeping: payment, email, shipping-order and order-publish legs were 0-4ms even in the outlier. The shippingservice-to-quoteservice hop looks like the top offender in fast traces at 5-6ms and 22-24% of trace time, but it is steady-state cost — only ~9ms in the slow trace. Currency, catalog and flag lookups were all 1.5ms or less. The "hung call with no span emitted" theory failed because the slow trace is fully populated to its terminal spans at ~+637ms. And narrowing to a pre-payment intake problem is wrong: the post-payment EmptyCart write stalls equally, so both read and write paths are affected.

## Branches that led nowhere, conclusion, and what is still open

Change history for checkoutservice over a full day is empty — no deploys, config edits or flag flips — ruling out both a same-service trigger and ongoing churn sustaining it. paymentservice has four entries, all automation-authored environment edits to the same telemetry export endpoint, in two matched set-then-revert cycles; the last revert landed about 1.1h before onset, so no net configuration delta carried into the incident and nothing on the payment request path was touched. Separately, the paymentservice error-ratio series returned no samples in either the incident or the baseline window; the absence predates the event by hours, so it is a missing or mislabelled series, not a symptom, and re-slicing it will not help.

Conclusion: checkoutservice is waiting, not failing. Cart storage operations became slow and orders hang in the cart phase. Suggested fix class is a restart of that path. Confidence medium, and the caveats matter more than the verdict: no query was ever run against cartservice or its storage, so blame rests entirely on span attribution seen from the checkout side; every trace returned predates onset (roughly 23:14-23:34) and only 5 of 30 came back before truncation, so the onset mechanism is inferred rather than observed; why the storage calls slowed is unsettled, which is exactly why a restart may not be the right remedy; and seven of ten services in the blast radius plus four unmeasured edges were never examined. If this recurs, go straight to cartservice's own metrics, logs and change history first.
