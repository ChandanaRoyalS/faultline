# Checkout PlaceOrder failing at the shipping-quote hop

## What was visible, in order

The page came from checkoutservice and the load generator at once: PlaceOrder failing. The initial radius listed ten services with four unmeasured edges, and nothing in it pointed at a specific hop.

The first metric pull was wide, roughly three hours. It showed error ratio elevated against baseline, but its change points landed mid-morning, around 09:17 and 09:56, nowhere near the stated 11:58 onset. That mismatch cost time and never held up. A narrower re-pull over the last half hour was far more legible: error ratio moved from about 1% to about 10%, a tenfold shift, peaks near 0.28, well outside baseline noise, with change points at roughly 11:36 and 11:56. Neither pull returned latency percentiles, CPU, memory, restarts, or in-flight counts, so saturation was never actually answered, only set aside.

Settled early and stayed settled: checkoutservice was not fully down, not crashlooping, and not cut off from traffic. The ratio never approached 1 and its minimum stayed at 0.

The logs sharpened things by absence rather than by an error string. No error- or exception-severity lines at all. At the window start, each PlaceOrder entry was followed within about twenty milliseconds by a payment confirmation, a confirmation-email line, and a successful message write. From roughly 11:57:48 onward only the entry lines remain: orders begin and nothing after them appears. Entry cadence stayed steady through 12:00:56 with no startup or shutdown lines, so the process was alive and accepting work while requests stalled between entry and payment.

## Dead ends worth keeping

cartservice was the obvious next suspect and produced nothing usable. Its metric query came back completely empty on both the incident window and the baseline, which reads as a naming or label mismatch rather than a behavior change. Record this plainly: an empty result is not a clean bill of health, and it removed the ability to confirm degradation and health alike. The cart logs were better, showing only routine read and add-item traces, no errors or connection failures, and steady multi-user activity into the final minute. The caveat is that only the oldest eight and newest thirty-two lines came back, leaving about thirty minutes of the middle unobserved.

The log evidence also closed several checkout-side theories: payment declines or duplicate charges (payment lines are absent entirely, not failing), the confirmation email or downstream publish (both sit after payment and were never reached), and a currency-specific problem (stalled orders covered both USD and CAD).

Two change-history queries were run and both were run wrong. Both were scoped to the seed service alone, zero hops, and both windows opened at the 11:58 onset and ran forward roughly twenty-four hours. The hours before onset, the only ones that could hold the triggering edit, were never covered. What they legitimately establish is narrow: no checkoutservice deploy, config edit, flag flip, rollout, or silent remediation during or after onset. That is not an answer to what changed.

## Where it landed and what is still open

The trace query resolved it in one look. In fourteen of fifteen PlaceOrder traces, the checkoutservice client span for the shipping-quote call is ERROR and that error propagates to the frontend root. It is fast: the hop completes in roughly 1.8 to 3.0 milliseconds with whole traces at 6 to 14 milliseconds, so this is immediate rejection and not a wait, which kills the timeout and saturation-latency theories. The shipping-quote span has no server-side child span from shippingservice at all, unlike cart, product catalog, and currency calls which each show a matching server span; the call is refused before shippingservice records work. Those three neighbours, plus cart's Redis HGET, all complete without error in well under a millisecond. paymentservice and emailservice never appear, because execution aborts during order preparation. One trace shows the same failure with no shipping-quote span emitted at all, only the cart lookup.

Conclusion, held at low confidence: failure at a single hop on the shipping path, consistent with a wrong endpoint or a process not listening where checkout expects it. The fix class recorded was a configuration revert, but the specific wrong value was inferred from the shape of the failure, never read.

Still open, in order of cost to close: read the status code and message on the failing shipping-quote span, which separates unreachable from a bad value or bad build; collect shippingservice logs, metrics, and traces, since we never established whether that process is up, restarting, or refusing connections; and re-run change history with the window opening at least an hour before onset and the radius expanded to include shippingservice. Note also that the error ratio is bursty with change points at roughly 11:36 and 11:56 rather than a clean step at 11:58, so true onset and whether the shipping path failed totally remain unclear.
