# Ablation E6 - dense-only retrieval (2026-10-15)

**T7.3, experiment E6, on the v2 catalog.** Registered in [`PREREGISTRATION-T7.3.md`](PREREGISTRATION-T7.3.md) before the build and before any run (the switch: Addendum 1; the queue and the nights: Addendum 3). The switch was proven to take effect in the switch trial ([`PREREGISTRATION-trial-v2.md`](PREREGISTRATION-trial-v2.md), Addendum 8).

**What varies.** Hybrid retrieval's text arm is switched off and only the dense arm is fused (`FAULTLINE_CONTEXT_RETRIEVAL_MODE=dense`). The trial recorded a fact about F that makes this experiment close to an A/A: **F's own retrieved hits were single-arm values**, so on that scenario the hybrid behaved as one arm would, and the trial registered that E6 may measure nothing against F.

## Reading it

**No measurable effect, as the trial anticipated.** The verdicts moved on several scenarios in
both directions, which is the run-to-run variance the A/A table shows for F against itself, and
the paired difference is null on fault class and inside the MDE on service. Cost and time did
not move measurably. **The registered prediction (cost within +-10 %) held.**

**The per-hit reading the trial registered** is in the block, from the trajectory store's
export. It confirms the trial's single-scenario fact across the batch: **F's hybrid almost never
had both arms agree on a chunk** - two of F's hits, on one scenario, were ranked by both the dense
and the text arm; every other hit was one arm's. So F's "hybrid" mostly behaves as the union of
two single-arm lists, cut to three. Switching the text arm off did change what was read - E6 and F
shared one to three documents per scenario, of the three to six distinct documents each read
across its two retrievals - and accuracy still did not move measurably. On this corpus the retriever's arm choice does not decide the verdict; that fits E5,
where removing retrieval altogether also showed no measurable effect.

## What this does not establish

- **A size.** Ten dev scenarios at R = 1 resolve nothing smaller than the MDE above, and the
  interval beside every difference says how little is pinned down. The plan asked for the full
  catalog at R = 5; the owner's scale (12 scenarios, then 11, then 10 after one was removed) is
  registered in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`.
- **Anything about holdout.** Dev scenarios only, by ADR-0008: an ablation's result is what tuning
  reads.
- **A control without noise.** F is one run per scenario. The A/A table in `docs/ABLATIONS.md`
  shows the same configuration changing its mark on half the scenarios between two runs nine days
  apart, so any single scenario's difference here can be noise; only the paired difference over
  all ten is read.
- **Anything about another world.** OTel Demo 2.2.0 on the Mac, at `cap:91279a09`; nothing here is
  about SREGym.
