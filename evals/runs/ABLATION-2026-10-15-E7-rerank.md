## Reading it

**No measurable effect on accuracy.** Reranking changed which documents the agents read and the
verdicts moved both ways - it fixed the product-catalog freeze's class and broke the ad memory
squeeze outright - and the paired difference is null on fault class and inside the MDE on service.

**It cost slightly less, not more**, with an interval that only just stays below zero. The
reranker itself is free (local); a lower total means the agents' own calls were cheaper, which a
different set of retrieved documents could plausibly cause, and which ten scenarios cannot
attribute. **The registered prediction (no more than 15 % higher) held**, on its upper bound.

E7 lost one scenario outright to the removal rule: `v2-cart-store-corruption` was discarded twice
under E7 (both times the incident had resolved before the investigation started), so that
scenario is out of every experiment and tabled apart in `docs/ABLATIONS.md`.

## What this does not establish

- **A size.** Ten dev scenarios at R = 1 resolve nothing smaller than the MDE above, and the
  interval beside every difference says how little is pinned down. The plan asked for the full
  catalog at R = 5; the owner's scale (12 scenarios, then 11, then 10 after one was removed) is
  registered in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`.
- **Anything about holdout.** Dev scenarios only, by ADR-0008: an ablation's result is what tuning
  reads.
- **A control without noise.** F is one run per scenario. The A/A table in `docs/ABLATIONS.md`
  shows the same configuration changing its mark on half the scenarios between two runs nine days
  apart, so any single scenario's difference here can be noise; only the paired difference over
  all ten is read.
- **Anything about another world.** OTel Demo 2.2.0 on the Mac, at `cap:91279a09`; nothing here is
  about SREGym.
