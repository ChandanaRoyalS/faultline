> **Correction, same day, to §5.** This document said of the thirty pre-flight refusals: *"**No
> published figure moved** — `evaldb.outcome_of` recovers the distinction from an absent
> `injected_at`."* **That is false, and the figure moved.** The recovery covers a manifest
> *labelled* a discard; these thirty carried no outcome label at all, and the fallthrough beneath
> that branch read `else "discarded"`. They were counted as discards, the recorded rate went from
> **16.7% to 28.6%**, and `tests/test_evaldb.py` failed on `main` within minutes of this document
> merging — which is how the claim was found to be wrong. The rate is now **18.2%**, stated over
> the 121 runs that started rather than all 182 directories, and asserted against the committed
> record so the constant and the tree cannot drift apart.
>
> **The error was mine to make and it was made the same way twice**: by reading the recovery's
> docstring and its first branch instead of its last line, and then writing the conclusion into
> two places. **Corrected here rather than edited away**, because a sweep document that quietly
> loses a false claim is the one thing a reader of a benchmark cannot check. §5 is left as it was

## 2. The predictions

### 1. Every scored run names a service — **FAILED, and not by the registered mechanism**

Registered: *"5 of 5 return a non-empty `service`… One empty service is a defect in the prompt, not
an abstention."*

**4 of 4 verdicts named a service, and every one was right.** The fifth run produced no verdict at
all, so there was no field to be empty. The registered failure was *the synthesizer declining to
answer a question its prompt asks*; the actual failure was *the contract rejecting an answer the
synthesizer gave*. Those are opposite defects and the prediction cannot be read as having caught
the second — it is scored failed on its letter and its diagnosis is set aside.

### 2. The pipeline names the culprit, not the propagator, on `ad-memory-squeeze` — **HELD**

Registered: **`adservice`, not `frontend`.** The sharpest prediction in the document, because a
`frontend` here would have meant five sweeps of `resource_exhaustion` ✔ were passing for the right
class and the wrong reason.

Triage started from `frontend`. The verdict named **`adservice`**.

The contrast is measurable rather than argued, because B0.2 ran the same five scenarios the same
morning at \$0.00 and took the trap on four of them, three times by way of *"earliest alerting
service (no error-ratio series available)"* — which picks `frontend`. **ADR-0020 §6's warning that
`start_from` is an entry point and not a culprit claim is now a measured distinction between two
arms rather than a note in an ADR.**

Nothing in the project's history needs re-reading. That is the result this prediction existed to
obtain, and it is the only one of the nine whose failure would have cost more than it did to test.

### 3. Top-3 adds almost nothing at this catalog size — **HELD, at zero**

Registered: `gained_by_ranking` true on **at most 1 of 5**, plausibly 0. **It is 0 of 4.**

### 4. `depth` is greater than 1 on most runs — **FAILED**

Registered: **at least 3 of 5 carry at least one alternative.** **1 of 4 did** —
`cart-dependency-latency`, which offered `redis-cart` behind `cartservice`, a genuinely considered
runner-up.

The registered consequence is applied rather than reconsidered: *"if the list is empty everywhere,
then top-3 equals top-1 by construction, the field is costing tokens for nothing, and the honest
response is to say the synthesizer does not rank rather than to publish a top-3 column."* The list
is not empty everywhere, it is empty on three of four. **Top-3 must not be published as a column at
this stamp.** `ScoredRun.report` now prints depth beside the rates and says *top-3 = top-1 by
construction* when depth is 1, so the distinction cannot be lost by a reader looking at two
identical hits.

**And this prediction was measured with its best sample deleted by a contract.**
`cart-bad-image-tag` returned **two** alternatives — more than any run that scored — and the
verdict failed validation because each carried a `remediation_class`. The run most likely to have
satisfied prediction 4 is the one the schema destroyed. Whatever the true ranking rate is, 1 of 4
is a floor and not an estimate.

### 5. Triage is byte-identical to sweep 8 — **HELD, 4 of 4**

| scenario | sweep 8 | sweep 9 |
|---|---|---|
| `ad-memory-squeeze` | 1.00 / 0.43 | **1.00 / 0.43** |
| `cart-dependency-latency` | 1.00 / 0.33 | **1.00 / 0.33** |
| `cart-redis-misconfig` | 0.80 / 0.67 | **0.80 / 0.67** |
| `frauddetection-memory-squeeze` | 1.00 / 1.00 | **1.00 / 1.00** |

One qualification the prediction's reasoning did not carry. It argued the traversal is
*"deterministic over a fixed graph"*, and the traversal is — but recall and precision are computed
against `alerts_over_window`, which is a property of the world on the day. `cart-bad-image-tag`
scored 1.00/0.71 in the pipeline arm and 0.80/0.67 in the B0.2 arm two hours earlier, on the same
deterministic traversal. **The determinism is in the prediction, not in the pair.** Four identical
rows is therefore a stronger result than the prediction's own argument entitled it to expect, and a
future difference on this axis would need the alert set checked before it is called a defect.

### 6. `cart-bad-image-tag` is registered as unsettled — **the clause does not fire**

Registered: no outcome predicted, but *"if it fails again, that is the second consecutive failure on
the only `bad_deploy` scenario this pipeline has measured, and it stops being an instance."*

**It did not fail again. It was never diagnosed.** The synthesizer produced a verdict with two
ranked alternatives and `Candidate` rejected it for an extra field, twice, so the run recorded no
fault class, no service and no narrative. Reading that as the second consecutive diagnosis failure
would attribute a harness defect to the pipeline's reasoning, which is the exact error the clause
was written to prevent in the other direction.

**`bad_deploy` remains a sample of one.** The scenario is still unsettled and the next
pre-registration inherits this clause unchanged.

### 7. B0.2 does not beat the pipeline, and its cost is \$0.00 — **HELD on cost, NOT A SEPARATION on accuracy**

| | fault class correct | answered | \$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| pipeline | **2** | 3 of 5 | 2.9949 |
| B0.2 | **1** | 5 of 5 | **0.0000** |

The zero is confirmed as a measurement: every B0.2 manifest records `$0.0000` rather than an absent
cost field, because B0 makes no model call and the pre-flight check is skipped without constructing
a client.

**The accuracy half must not be read as a separation.** Two correct against one, at n=5 with four
scored verdicts, against a minimum detectable effect of roughly 28 percentage points at n=10 —
nothing here distinguishes the arms on fault class. What *does* distinguish them, and sharply, is
the service axis: B0.2 took the propagator trap four times out of five and the pipeline took it
zero times out of four. That is the comparison worth making from this sweep, and it is a comparison
the benchmark could not have made before T4.2.

B0.2 also answered on all five where the pipeline answered on three. **A heuristic that always
guesses has a coverage advantage over a system permitted to abstain**, and reading coverage as
competence would invert the ranking. ADR-0022 §1.2 exists for this.

### 8. Cost — **FAILED, low**

Registered **\$3.50–\$5.00**; actual **\$2.9949**, about 17% below the floor. Registered on the
premise that the alternatives field lengthens one reply. It does — where the field is used, and
three of four verdicts left it empty, which is prediction 4's failure showing up in the budget. The
five runs also ran cheaper per-run than sweep 8's \$0.70 average: \$0.60 counting all five, \$0.65
excluding the run that died early.

### 9. Discards below 10% — **HELD, at zero**

Five scenarios, five scored, no discards. §5 covers the thirty refusals that preceded them, which
are not discards and are not counted here.

## 3. The result the two axes disagree about

On `ad-memory-squeeze` the pipeline named the right service and **abstained** on the class. On
`cart-redis-misconfig` it named the right service and got the class **wrong**. On the other two it
got both.

The judge's trap column explains the first. `ad-memory-squeeze`'s narrative **took** the trap
*"process death from outside looks like connection failure on the client side"* — it saw a service
refusing gRPC connections and reasoned about reachability rather than about a process being killed
for exceeding a limit. Its own verdict says so:

> *"No dispatch ever examined adservice itself, so why it is unreachable — process down,
> crash-looping, saturated accept queue, or wrong bind address — remains undetermined, and I leave
> fault_class unknown rather than guess."*

**It knew what it did not know and declined.** The scorer records that identically to a confident
wrong answer — as an abstention, excluded from accuracy, counted in coverage — and the judge grades
it `different` by construction, because there is no claim to compare. Both behaviours are correct
under their own rules and together they make an honest abstention look like the worst row in the
table. That is a property of the instruments, not of the run, and it is stated here so nobody
reading the ledger later concludes `ad-memory-squeeze` regressed.

## 5. The full ledger of the day

**35 run directories**, of which 5 scored.

| | count | what they are |
|---|---|---|
| scored | 5 | this sweep |
| refused, pre-flight | 30 | six attempts each on five scenarios, before the key was exported |

The thirty refusals are **not discards and are not in any rate**. Q20's pre-flight check refused
before the baseline gate and therefore before the freeze and before injection, so nothing was
injected and the world was untouched on every one of them. That is the check working exactly as its
queue row proposed: dev sweep 8 injected one scenario four times before discovering the same class
of failure at the triage call.

Two things about them are defects and are recorded rather than smoothed over.

**They carried no `REFUSED.md`.** The patch that established a refusal is not a discard taught
`except gate.GateRefusedError` to call `Run.refuse` and left `except preflight.PreflightError`
saving a manifest and returning. Thirty directories landed carrying neither the marker file nor a
`refused` key. **No published figure moved** — `evaldb.outcome_of` recovers the distinction from an
absent `injected_at` — which means a second mechanism was covering for a missing first, and that is
the part worth writing down.

**Six attempts apiece was twenty-five minutes of asking an unanswerable question.** The refusal was
*"Could not resolve authentication method"* — a missing credential, not a settling world — and the
sweep retried it sixty seconds apart, six times, five times over. The exit code is 3 either way, so
the code could not tell them apart. Both are fixed; §6.

## 6. What this sweep found that is not a result

Five defects, all found by running things that had been green and unexercised.

| # | defect | fixed in |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Candidate` forbids extra fields while `SYNTHESIZER_SYSTEM` primes one its schema block omits — cost one run of five | **queued as Q25**: the fix moves `prompt_digest` |
| 2 | `ScoredRun.report` scored `service` and `ranked_service` and printed neither | #198 |
| 3 | `PreflightError` never called `Run.refuse` | #198 |
| 4 | the sweep re-asked a refusal its own evidence showed was standing | #198 |
| 5 | `faultline-judge` with no arguments re-judges and **overwrites all 79 judged runs**; its cost line billed a Haiku judge at Opus rates | #197 |

Defect 5 is the serious one. Rewriting captured evidence is the rule this repository does not bend,
and the default invocation of the repository's own tool did it — not through a bug, but through the
default. It was found because an unset judge model refused first.

Defect 1 is the expensive one and it is not fixed here on purpose. `Candidate` is reachable from
`Verdict.model_json_schema()`, which is in `stamp._CONTRACTS`, so changing it moves the stamp off
`ba8684b01201`. **The comparability cost of that move is four scored runs — this sweep — and it
will never be smaller.** Q25 records three routes and recommends adding `remediation_class` to the
candidate rather than ignoring it, on the grounds that the model's instinct was right: an
alternative root cause with no proposed fix is half a claim.

## 7. What this sweep cannot say

**It cannot separate Batch C's effects from T4.2's.** The stamp moved twice since sweep 8 —
`bc222a353936` → `7c6894e9dd92` (Batch C) → `ba8684b01201` (T4.2) — and neither intermediate stamp
was ever run. Nothing here should be attributed to either change individually. The pre-registration
said this in advance and it is repeated because a reader comparing by stamp alone will get it
wrong.

**Q23's planner retrieval moved behaviour and moved no digest.** The planner now receives the top-3
similar past incidents that T3.2 always specified. `prompt_digest` covers system prompts and
contract schemas; a briefing's contents are neither. **A reader attributing any planner difference
between sweeps 8 and 9 to the T4.2 prompt move would be wrong.** The fact of retrieval is checkable
per run in `trajectory_retrievals`.

**It cannot rate anything.** Four scored verdicts. n=1 per scenario. CLAUDE.md rule 6 applies to
every number above, and `MIN_N_FOR_VERDICT = 3` means the variance machinery refuses to issue a
verdict on this at all.

**The judge is not calibrated.** 5 blind human grades against the ~30 T4.2 asks for, and the judged
table says so on every printing. Every agreement figure here is one model's opinion of another
model's prose, and a figure that turns out to disagree with a human audit is withdrawn, not
footnoted.

**Nothing was re-run.** One run per scenario, no re-runs, no discards to replace. The one scenario
that produced nothing stays as it is.
