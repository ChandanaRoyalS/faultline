## Reading it

**No measurable effect, and the sign is the predicted one.** The registration said that if a
difference showed it would be negative; both axes came out slightly lower than F's, well inside
the MDE.

**The cost prediction did not hold**: unbounded windows were expected to cost at least 10 % more,
and where neither arm was gated they cost the same. That is consistent with the registration's
own caveat - the tools cap what they return by lines and spans, so a week of logs reaches the
model as the same capped page an incident window does. The scoping's value on this world is
therefore not in tokens; whether it is in what the capped page contains is what the accuracy
axis would show at a size that can see it.

E8 was gated on one more scenario than F; triage runs before any tool, so that is not the switch.

## What this does not establish

- **A size.** Ten dev scenarios at R = 1 resolve nothing smaller than the MDE above, and the
  interval beside every difference says how little is pinned down. The plan asked for the full
  catalog at R = 5; the owner's scale (12 scenarios, then 11, then 10 after one was removed) is
  registered in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`.
- **Anything about holdout.** Dev scenarios only, by ADR-0008: an ablation's result is what tuning
  reads.
- **A control without noise.** F is one run per scenario. The A/A table in `docs/ABLATIONS.md`
  shows the same configuration changing its mark on half the scenarios between two runs nine days
  apart, so any single scenario's difference here can be noise; only the paired difference over
  all ten is read.
- **Anything about another world.** OTel Demo 2.2.0 on the Mac, at `cap:91279a09`; nothing here is
  about SREGym.
