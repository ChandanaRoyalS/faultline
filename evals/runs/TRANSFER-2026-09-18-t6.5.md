## 3. The within-arm test, which is the one that settles it

Prediction 4 exists to separate *learning* from *retrieval*. Measured off
`trajectory_retrievals`: **18 of 30 WITH-arm runs surfaced a same-class document** — more than
half, so prediction 4 failed, and the retriever is doing better than §5 feared.

That makes the decisive comparison available, and it is **inside the WITH arm**, where nothing
differs but whether retrieval happened to find a class-mate:

| | correct | abstained | wrong | accuracy (ab. excluded) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| same-class document **surfaced** (n=18) | 12 | 4 | 2 | 12/14 — 85.7% |
| **none surfaced** (n=12) | 8 | 2 | 2 | 8/10 — 80.0% |

**Counted the way the headline is counted, both are 66.7%** — 12/18 and 8/12, identical to three
significant figures. Abstentions excluded, surfacing is 5.7pp *better*, which is well inside the
noise and points the opposite way to the arm delta.

**So the thing the arms vary did not move the answer.** A WITH-arm run that actually read a
same-class prior incident did no better and no worse than one that did not.

**And `cart-bad-image-tag` proves it directly.** It carries the whole −3, and a same-class
document surfaced in **only 1 of its 3 WITH runs**. Two of its three failures happened with a
retrievable corpus that differed from the WITHOUT arm's by two documents neither run read. Those
runs cannot have been harmed by a document they never saw.

**A mechanism was proposed and is withdrawn.** On first reading §2 it looked like a retrieved
class-mate pulling the agent toward the retrieved incident's answer. §3 is what that hypothesis
predicts and it is not there.

## 4. What this is, then

**Run-to-run variance, at a scale the floors were written for.** Three runs per cell makes 0/3
against 3/3 an ordinary outcome, and sweep 12's A/A check measured **+10.0pp on fault class
between two halves of one configuration** — a difference produced by nothing at all, larger than
the 9.3pp measured here between two arms that differ on purpose.

**Prediction 3 held by 0.7pp against a floor estimated at "~10pp", and that margin is not
meaningful.** The honest statement is that the delta and the instrument's own noise are the same
size, not that the delta is smaller.

**Prediction 5's failure is the residue worth keeping.** §7 registered that less context should
produce *more* declining, and the opposite happened: **the WITH arm abstained twice as often**,
6 against 3, and every extra abstention sits on a scenario whose accuracy also fell. Whatever the
larger corpus did, it made the agent less willing to commit rather than better informed. That is
a finding about behaviour and it survives the variance argument in a way the accuracy delta does
not, because it is in the same direction on every scenario where anything moved at all.

## 5. The reporting obligations from Amendments 4 and 5

**Over all ten donors:** −9.3pp (abstentions excluded), −16.7pp (counted wrong).

**Over the nine donors that did not answer wrongly — post-hoc, and labelled so.** Excluding
`ad-memory-squeeze`, whose postmortem Amendment 4 recorded as wrong: **WITH 19/27, WITHOUT 23/27,
−14.8pp** counted wrong. Removing the one document known to be wrong makes the WITH arm look
*worse*, not better, which is the opposite of what excluding it was expected to do and is another
reason to read §3 rather than the arm difference.

**`frauddetection-memory-squeeze`, named whichever way the delta fell** (Amendment 4 §4): **3/3
in both arms, delta 0.** It is the scenario whose WITH arm carried `ad-memory-squeeze`'s wrong
postmortem, and it answered correctly every time in both arms. The contamination Amendment 4
registered as a risk did not cost a single run.

## 6. What this does not say

**Not that prior incidents do not help.** §8 already bounded this: it is a measurement *through
this retriever*, and Amendment 6 moved the attenuation bound to `recall@3 = 0.302` — worse than
the 0.395 §5 was written against, because the ten postmortems displace the golden set's answers.
§3's within-arm test is the stronger evidence here and it says *no effect when retrieval works*,
which is a narrower and better-supported claim than the arm delta.

**Not that postmortems are worse than narratives.** §8: the two arms cannot separate them, and
the WITH arm carries both.

**Not that the WITHOUT arm is better.** Reading a −9.3pp delta as *"withholding same-class
documents improves accuracy"* would be the same error as reading it the other way, and §3 is why.

**Not a claim about the holdout.** Dev only, as registered.
