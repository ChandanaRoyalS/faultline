# Retrieval, measured twice — Q39, and the first before/after this repository can compute

**2026-09-13.** The text arm is disjunctive. This is what changed, and it is not what the
registration predicted.

| | recall@5 | MRR@5 | | recall@3 | MRR@3 |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 2026-09-12, one-armed | **0.465** | 0.219 | | 0.256 | 0.159 |
| 2026-09-13, two-armed | **0.442** | **0.311** | | **0.395** | **0.233** |

**`recall@5` went down.** `PREREGISTRATION-Q39.md` §3 registered it as the number that decides, so
the rule as written says do not adopt. It was adopted anyway, and §4 below is the whole argument
for that, including the part that is uncomfortable.

Both measurements ran against the same 263-chunk corpus on the same machine, with the same golden
set, the same embedder and the same `RRF_K`. **$0.00**, as prediction 10 registered.

---

## 1. The change

`plainto_tsquery` ANDs every lexeme. A chunk had to contain all of a query's terms to match, and
real queries name several services and a symptom that no one chunk holds — so **all 43 golden
queries matched zero rows on the text arm**, `fuse()` fused one list, and `RRF_K` was a constant
over a single arm. That had been true since T2.4b.

The arm now takes `plainto_tsquery`'s own lexemes and joins them with `|`. Stemming, stopword
removal and quoting are Postgres's, unchanged; the operator is the only thing that moved.

**The other candidate was removed before any of this was measured.** §1.2 of the registration
records it: `websearch_to_tsquery` also matches 0 of 43, producing byte-identical output on 21
queries and differing on the other 22 only by treating hyphenated compounds as phrase constraints,
which is stricter. Q39's queue row named two candidates and there was one.

## 3. The ten predictions

**Six held, four failed.**

| # | registered | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | text arm non-empty for all 43; ≥ 5 chunks for ≥ 42 | **held** — 43 of 43 |
| 2 | `recall@5` rises above 0.465 | **failed** — 0.442 |
| 3 | rises by 0.02–0.15, landing in [0.485, 0.615] | **failed** |
| 4 | does not clear the 0.60 floor | held — but by falling, not by rising short |
| 5 | `MRR@5` rises by less than `recall@5` does | **failed, inverted** — MRR +0.092, recall −0.023 |
| 6 | `MRR@5` does not clear 0.45 | held — 0.311 |
| 7 | synthesizer improves more than planner | **failed** — synth −0.091, planner +0.047 |
| 8 | at least one partition gets worse at `recall@5` | held — four of five did |
| 9 | corpus-age gap does not close below 0.10 | held — it **widened**, 0.269 → 0.295 |
| 10 | $0.00 | held |

**Prediction 7 is the instructive one.** It was registered with its own counter-argument attached:
*"synthesizer queries are long, and a long query ORs into the widest match sets, so dilution should
hurt them most. If prediction 7 fails, that is the reading to take."* It failed, and that is the
reading. The synthesizer role is the only place this change does clear harm — 6 of 22 down to 4 of
22 at k=5 — and it is harmed by exactly the mechanism named in advance.

**Prediction 4 held for the opposite of the registered reason**, and counting it as a success
without saying so would be the cheapest kind of scoring. It predicted the pipeline would improve
but not enough; the pipeline did not improve on that metric at all.

## 4. The decision, and two defects in the rule that made it

### 4.1 What the rule said

§3: *"The number that decides is `recall@5` over all 43 queries."* It fell. The rule says the
change loses. **The change was adopted anyway.** Here is why, with the weakest part first.

### 4.2 The whole difference is one query

`recall@5` went from 20 of 43 to 19 of 43. One query.

One query out of 43 is **0.0233**. §3's tie-break reads *"if both are within 0.02, the change is
reported as no measured difference"* — and 0.02 is **smaller than the smallest difference this
instrument can express.** A recall difference over 43 queries is either exactly zero or at least
0.0233. The band was unreachable by construction.

This is arithmetic about the instrument, not a reading of the result: it would have been true if
the number had moved the other way, and it was true when the band was written. The clause's evident
intent — ignore differences at the noise floor — is satisfied by a one-query difference. Applying
that intent is the argument for adoption.

**It is also convenient, and pretending otherwise would be worse than the defect.** A rule was
found wanting at exactly the moment it ruled against a change I had written and expected to work.
The mitigations are that the defect is arithmetic rather than interpretive, that it is stated here
in full, and that the decision is recorded as the owner's, taken after seeing the data.

### 4.3 The deciding metric measures a configuration nothing runs

§3 chose `recall@5` *"because that is what the registered floor is stated in and what the CI gate
reads."* Both true, and both facts about the **reporting apparatus** rather than about the
pipeline. Production retrieves `k = 3`.

At k = 3 there is no ambiguity in either direction: recall 0.256 → 0.395, MRR 0.159 → 0.233,
eleven queries answered becoming seventeen. T6.4 already flagged this mismatch — the `--k` help
text says 3 is production's and 5 is what the floor was written against, *"which are not the same
pipeline"* — and then registered the floor's k as decisive anyway. **Q47** carries it.

This is not offered as the reason for adoption, because choosing a different metric after seeing
which one favours you is the thing the apparatus exists to prevent. It is offered as the reason the
rule deserves to be rewritten **before** the next measurement, where it can be registered honestly.

### 4.4 The gate's recalibration rule has the same shape of problem

§3.1 registered recalibration at *the same relative margin below the measurement* — 14.0% for
recall, which on 0.442 gives **0.38**. The existing gate is 0.40.

**A relative-margin rule lowers the gate whenever the measurement falls.** That converts a
regression into the new baseline, which is the single thing a regression gate exists to prevent.
So the rule applied was the tighter of the registered formula and the constant already standing:
`MRR` takes the formula's tightening, 0.18 → **0.26**; `recall` keeps **0.40**, still below the
0.442 measured.

That deviation was also decided after seeing the data. What distinguishes it from back-fitting is
direction — it is strictly more demanding than what was registered, in both metrics, so it cannot
flatter the change it ships with.

`test_the_gate_is_below_the_measurement_and_the_measurement_is_below_the_floor` now asserts the
ordering for **both** metrics. It had only ever asserted recall, and nobody noticed until the
constants moved.

### 4.5 Where the pipeline stands

Still below its own registered floor, on both metrics and at both k. 0.442 against 0.60; 0.311
against 0.45. The floor does not move, for the reason §3.1 gives: it was argued before any
measurement existed, and a floor that follows the measurement is not a floor.

## 5. What this does not settle

- **Whether retrieval helps the agent.** Unchanged from T6.4 §5. `recall` says the right document
  is reachable, not that reading it changes a verdict.
- **Whether the synthesizer regression matters in production.** At k = 3, the configuration
  production runs, synthesizer recall is flat (4 of 22 both times) and only `MRR` falls. At k = 5
  it loses two queries. **Q49.**
- **Whether `RRF_K = 60` is right.** It has never been tuned, and it is now for the first time a
  constant over two real lists rather than one — newly meaningful and still unmeasured.
- **Whether an OR arm is the right design.** It is the arm that could be measured against what
  exists. BM25, field weighting, a learned sparse retriever — none is ruled out, and this
  measurement is what such a proposal now has to beat.
- **Whether the corpus is the right corpus.** Both measurements ran against 263 chunks where the
  seeder produces 261. Q40's reconciliation landed in code and this database has not been re-seeded
  since. Holding it fixed was deliberate — one change at a time — and it means neither published
  figure is against a corpus that matches its own source files. **Q48.**
