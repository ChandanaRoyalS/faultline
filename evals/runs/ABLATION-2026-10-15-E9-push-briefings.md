# Ablation E9 - push-everything briefings (2026-10-15)

**T7.3, experiment E9, on the v2 catalog.** Registered in [`PREREGISTRATION-T7.3.md`](PREREGISTRATION-T7.3.md) before the build and before any run (the switch: Addendum 1; the queue and the nights: Addendum 3). The switch was proven to take effect in the switch trial ([`PREREGISTRATION-trial-v2.md`](PREREGISTRATION-trial-v2.md), Addendum 8).

**What varies.** Every briefed role gets every briefing section in full at the start, with no budget and nothing left to pull (`FAULTLINE_AGENT_BRIEFING_MODE=push`). The trial showed about three times the pushed tokens and a lower pull rate than F. **The specialists are not briefed** (each gets a dispatch question), so push reaches the five briefed roles only (`docs/DEVIATIONS.md`).

## Reading it

**No measurable effect on accuracy, and a cost that is plainly higher.** Pushing everything
changed verdicts in both directions - it got the product-catalog freeze and the valkey latency
right on both axes where F had not, and lost the ad partition outright, answered the bad config
wrongly where F had abstained, and lost the fraud-detection flag lag to a gate - and nets to
nothing on either axis.

**The cost is where this size can see something.** On the scenarios where neither arm was gated,
push cost about a quarter more, with an interval clear of zero, and **the registered prediction
(at least 20 % higher) held on that reading**. On all ten it did not: E9 was gated on one more
scenario than F, and a gated run costs almost nothing, which drags E9's total down for a reason
that is not its briefing. Both readings are in the block.

So progressive disclosure is doing what it was built for - holding the briefing down - and costs
nothing measurable in accuracy at this size.

## What this does not establish

- **A size.** Ten dev scenarios at R = 1 resolve nothing smaller than the MDE above, and the
  interval beside every difference says how little is pinned down. The plan asked for the full
  catalog at R = 5; the owner's scale (12 scenarios, then 11, then 10 after one was removed) is
  registered in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`.
- **Anything about holdout.** Dev scenarios only, by ADR-0008: an ablation's result is what tuning
  reads.
- **A control without noise.** F is one run per scenario. The A/A table in `docs/ABLATIONS.md`
  shows the same configuration changing its mark on half the scenarios between two runs nine days
  apart, so any single scenario's difference here can be noise; only the paired difference over
  all ten is read.
- **Anything about another world.** OTel Demo 2.2.0 on the Mac, at `cap:91279a09`; nothing here is
  about SREGym.
