# Ablation E2 - topology scoping off (2026-10-15)

**T7.3, experiment E2, on the v2 catalog.** **Registered** in [`PREREGISTRATION-T7.3.md`](PREREGISTRATION-T7.3.md) (Addendum 1: the
switch `FAULTLINE_CONTEXT_HOP_RADIUS=99`, larger than the v2 graph's diameter, so the blast radius,
the planner's brief and the proposal check would cover the whole graph). **Not run**, by the
owner's decision of 2026-10-06 ([`PREREGISTRATION-trial-v2.md`](PREREGISTRATION-trial-v2.md),
Addendum 8), and in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`. The registration provides for this: the report is written
from the computed radii.

## Why there is nothing to run

The switch was built, recorded in the manifest and tried once. On the trial's page the blast
radius at 99 hops held the same services as at 2. That is not a broken switch: **the blast radius
grows upward**, from the alerting service to its callers (ADR-0017), and almost every page in this
catalog already alerts at or near the top of the call graph, where no larger radius reaches more.

## Reading it

**On this world, topology scoping cannot be told apart from no scoping**, because a radius of 2
already reaches everything the graph reaches from almost every page. Running E2 would have been
about $11 for runs that are, scenario for scenario, the same configuration as F - ten A/A runs and
one scenario, and that one is `v2-payment-memory-squeeze`, which the batch could not run at all.

**That is the finding**, and it is a finding about the world as much as the system: the OTel demo
is a shallow graph whose alerts fire at the edge. On a deeper service graph, or with alert rules
that page on interior services, scoping would have something to prune and this experiment would
mean something. The switch stays built and recorded for that day.

## What this does not establish

That topology scoping is useless. It establishes that this world gives it nothing to do.
