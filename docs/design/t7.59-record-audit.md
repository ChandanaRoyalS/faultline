# T7.59 — reading the published record as a stranger

**An audit, not a rewrite.** README, RESULTS.md and the ADR set were amended incrementally across
this whole arc; each amendment was right when made and nobody had read the result as a whole. Every
claim was checked against the current state of the repo. **Fifteen corrections, all of them either
a wrong sentence or a missing one. No section was restructured**

> **Correction, T7.60: twelve of the fifteen reached the file, not fifteen.** The edits were applied in batches, and the batch carrying **C7** (the sweep-6 heading), **C8** (six → seven freeze items) and **C9** (the contradicted re-entry sentence) raised on a later assertion and **never wrote**. The remaining twelve landed. **The one that did not land is the one this document calls the worst error found** — which is its own small lesson: an audit that reports its corrections without re-reading the file it corrected is doing the thing it was auditing. **All three were applied at T7.60**, C7 as part of the restructure that removes the position the stale heading held. — T7.47's lesson stands and the
front door must not grow into a manual.

## 2. The stranger's question: what does this measure, and how much should I trust it?

**Trust: answered, and answered well.** README's *What these numbers are not* states the n, says a
95% interval spans most of the unit interval, and says the tables support direction and not
magnitude. Every judged figure carries `SHARED LINEAGE`. Abstention is explained and coverage is
never quoted apart from accuracy. **After C13 a reader can also see how much evidence exists and on
which world**, which was the one thing missing.

**What it measures: answered in pieces, and the pieces are not in one place.** The scored answer is
a **fault class and a class of fix**; the judge assesses the **mechanism named in the narrative**;
and T7.44 established that **warrant — whether the evidence cited actually supports the conclusion —
is assessed nowhere.** T7.52 measured what the first two can and cannot see: over 56 answered runs,
class accuracy and judged agreement are the same number, 52/56, **but not the same 52 runs** — six
disagree, three in each direction. That is the single most useful thing a stranger could be told
about the figures, **and it is in `docs/evidence/t7.52-corpus-judge/` and not in RESULTS.md.**

## 3. Structural gaps — recorded, not fixed on my own judgement

Following T7.46: where the gap is structural rather than a wrong sentence, write it down.

**G1 — RESULTS.md has no dev sweep 7 section.** Sweeps 4, 5 and 6 each get a full section with
tables; **the current benchmark exists only as a bullet in the opening banner.** The document's body
is a history of superseded results with the current one in a preamble. C7 removes the false claim
that sweep 6 is current, but it cannot supply the missing section — **writing one is restructuring
the results document**, which T7.47 says is not a correction to make on my own judgement.

**G2 — T7.52's label-versus-judge comparison is not in RESULTS.md.** T7.52 deliberately recommended
leaving the figures alone, and that recommendation was right about the *figures*. It left open
whether the *finding* belongs in the results document. It is the answer to "what can neither number
see", and it currently lives only in an evidence directory.

**G3 — "what does this benchmark measure" has no single home.** It is spread across ADR-0022 §1.2
(class settled by which fix works), T7.44 (warrant assessed nowhere) and T7.52 (where label and
judge disagree). Each is correct; a stranger has to assemble them.

**All three are the same shape**: the front door and the results document are organised by *when
things were measured* rather than by *what a reader needs first*. That is a reorganisation with an
argument behind it, not an audit's business.

## 4. The judgement call, made rather than deferred

**Does this arc's closing state deserve a short statement where a reader lands?**

**Yes — and not as a new summary.** The existing documents did carry the pieces, but *What remains*
was actively misleading: it listed permanently-closed limits in the same list as open questions, so
a reader met "the catalog is thirteen" and "entry 4" as work someone might still do. **The honest fix
was to correct that list rather than add a section that would duplicate it** (C14), plus the four
lines in README's holdout section that a reader needs before they draw the wrong conclusion (C3).

**Nothing new was created.** No new page, no new banner, no summary document. The front door is four
sentences longer than it was, and every one of them replaces an expectation the record was creating
by omission.
