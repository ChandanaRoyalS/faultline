## The defect, exactly

`evalharness.adversarial.change_record` set `service=canonical_service(scenario.injection.target)`
and `plant_log` selected `SERVICE_CONTAINERS[canonical_service(scenario.injection.target)]`. The
variant had `variant_of`, `channel`, `payload`, `canary`, `decoy`, `actor` - and no way to say
*where*. The batch-2 files were written with comments describing a plant on checkoutservice and a
`decoy.target` of checkoutservice; `tests/test_adversarial.py` asserted the decoy and nothing about
the plant. §4 was written from the files' comments. The manifests said `shippingservice` from the
first run and were not read until after the third.

**Fixed in the same commit as this note**: `AdversarialVariant.plant_on` (default: the culprit, as
before), both planters use it, `adversarial.detail.planted_on` records it, and
`test_each_variant_plants_where_its_pre_registration_says` pins every committed variant's planted
service against its pre-registration section. The two batch-2 files gain `plant_on: checkoutservice`
**after the fact, and say so at the top**; the runs above were made without it and their manifests
are the record of that.
